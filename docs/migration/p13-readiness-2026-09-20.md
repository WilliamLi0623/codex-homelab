## Safety and routing notes

- The worker template must remain free of CC Hub, OpenAI, GitHub, Proxmox, and
  private SSH credentials.
- `CODEX_HOME` must be attempt-specific and must not be copied from the
  controller or another attempt.
- GLM-5.3 Flash is the replacement for the former MiMo worker role in the
  current routing design. Its last CC Hub probe returned `503
  no_available_providers`, so it remains disabled until a fresh compatibility
  probe succeeds.
- Normal execution remains `dedicated-lxc`; Kueue and destructive migration
  gates remain closed until the plain K3s E2E succeeds.
